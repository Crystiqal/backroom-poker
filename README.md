# Back Room Poker

3D poker for friends. Play here: **https://crystiqal.github.io/backroom-poker/**

1. Open the link, type your name, press **Create a table**.
2. Send your friends the invite link (Copy, Share or the QR code).
3. They open it, type a name, press **Sit down**. No sign-up.

Keys: F fold, C check/call, R raise (Enter to confirm), A all-in, T chat, ? all shortcuts.

How it works: the game runs in the players' browsers. Messages go through free public relays
(Nostr and MQTT), end-to-end encrypted with a key that only exists in your invite link.
Hole cards are encrypted per player, and every shuffle can be checked afterwards (Menu → Rules & disputes → Audit a hand).
Play chips only, no real money.
